1. What Laiškelis is

The App lets you write short letters that are delivered to random other users. The core ideas:

  • Users only ever see each other's nicknames — there are no real names in the App.
  • A letter travels to random recipients; likes accompany its journey, and negative votes can end it early.
  • A letter expires after 7 days. Once expired, and only after a manual moderator review, it may be published in the public "Obituary" section.
  • A recipient may ask the author to start a private conversation. The conversation only exists if the author accepts.
  • A second kind of letter — the map letter — is pinned to a spot you choose on a map of Lithuania. Any signed-in user can read it, it lives for 30 days, and every rule in these Terms applies to it identically. The spot is always chosen by tapping the map; the App never reads your device's location.

The App is free and non-commercial: no ads, no purchases, no subscriptions.

  • You can hide any letter from yourself — it disappears from your Obituary list or your map immediately. The author is not told, and hiding is not a report.
  • You can delete your own letter at any time in the App — it stops travelling and disappears from other people's view immediately.
